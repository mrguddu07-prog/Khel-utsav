Our Story

ABOUT US

Khel Utsav: A Celebration of Sportsmanship at DBUU. The campus of Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University buzzes with immense energy and enthusiasm during the annual 'Khel Utsav.' This sports extravaganza serves as a vibrant platform for students to showcase their athletic prowess, discipline, and team spirit. Outdoor arenas fill with excitement as teams battle it out in Cricket, Football, Kabaddi, Basketball, and Volleyball. The spirit of competition is equally palpable indoors with Chess, Badminton, and Carrom. Set against the scenic backdrop of Uttarakhand, Khel Utsav is more than just a competition — it is a celebration of perseverance and camaraderie.
